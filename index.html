✌️ About me

Based in Los Angeles, I build applications where reliability and usability matter. I’m motivated by shipping products and seeing people succeed with the tools I create.

When I’m not working, I’m watching football 🗣️ Fight On!, wrenching on my motorcycle, nerding out on design systems, or catching up on the latest tech news.

⚒️ Building philosophy

I approach every project like a product owner — thinking through the entire experience: what users need, how the design flows, how the architecture scales, and how the small details add up to something polished and complete.

For me, it’s about turning “oh no” moments into “oh yeah!” experiences.

Above all, I stay curious — reflecting on what works, what doesn’t, and how to make every version better than the last.
